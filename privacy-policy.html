Privacy Policy

Jocelyn G. Capistrano, DDS

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Introduction

Jocelyn G. Capistrano, DDS ("the Practice," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and disclosed in connection with your use of the website located at capistranodental.info (the "Website"). This Privacy Policy should be read together with our Terms of Use, available at capistranodental.info/terms-of-use. By accessing or using the Website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies only to information collected through the Website and to communications you initiate with the Practice using the contact information listed on the Website. It does not govern the collection, use, or disclosure of protected health information in connection with dental care provided by the Practice. Protected health information of patients is handled in accordance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 ("HIPAA"), the California Confidentiality of Medical Information Act, other applicable law, and the Practice's Notice of Privacy Practices, which is available upon request at the office.

3. Information We Collect

The Website does not require you to register, create an account, or submit any personal information in order to view its content, and the Website does not contain forms for the submission of personal information.

Information You Provide Directly. If you choose to contact the Practice by telephone or email using the contact information listed on the Website, we will receive the information you choose to provide, which may include your name, telephone number, email address, and the contents of your message.

Information Collected Automatically. When you visit the Website, the servers that host the Website may automatically record certain technical information, such as your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, operating system, referring web page, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit. This information is collected as a standard function of website hosting and is used for purposes of operating, maintaining, and securing the Website.

4. Cookies, Tracking Technologies, and Third-Party Services

The Practice does not use cookies, web beacons, analytics services, or advertising technologies on the Website to track your activity.

Google Fonts. The Website uses Google Fonts, a web font service provided by Google LLC, to display text. When you visit the Website, your browser connects to Google's servers to load these fonts. As a result, Google may receive certain technical information from your browser, such as your IP address, browser type, and the address of the Website page you are viewing. The Practice does not control how Google collects, uses, or retains this information. For more information, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Other than as described above, the Practice does not knowingly permit third parties to collect personally identifiable information about your online activities over time or across different websites when you use the Website. If the Practice begins using additional third-party services or tracking technologies in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. How We Use Information

The Practice uses the information described above to respond to your inquiries and communications; to schedule or confirm appointments at your request; to operate, maintain, and secure the Website; and to comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. How We Disclose Information

The Practice does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. The Practice does not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing purposes. The Practice may disclose information only as follows: to service providers, such as website hosting and email service providers, that perform services on the Practice's behalf; to third-party services used to operate the Website, such as Google Fonts, as described in Section 4; when required by law, subpoena, court order, or other legal process; when necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of the Practice, its patients, or others; and in connection with the sale, transfer, or reorganization of the Practice, subject to applicable law.

7. Electronic Communications

Email and other electronic communications are not necessarily secure and may be intercepted or accessed by unauthorized persons. You should not send detailed personal health information, insurance identification numbers, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive information to the Practice by email. Please contact the Practice by telephone to discuss sensitive matters.

8. Data Security

The Practice uses reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information within its control. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and the Practice cannot guarantee the absolute security of any information.

9. Data Retention

The Practice retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, or as required or permitted by applicable law, including laws governing the retention of patient records.

10. Reviewing and Correcting Your Information

If you have provided personal information to the Practice through the contact methods listed on the Website and wish to review, correct, or request deletion of that information, please contact the Practice using the information in Section 15. The Practice will respond to your request as required by applicable law. Requests relating to patient records are governed by the Practice's Notice of Privacy Practices and applicable law.

11. Do Not Track Signals

Some web browsers offer a "Do Not Track" setting. Because the Practice does not track visitors over time or across third-party websites, the Website does not respond differently to Do Not Track signals.

12. Your California Privacy Rights

California Civil Code Section 1798.83 permits California residents to request information regarding the disclosure of their personal information to third parties for the third parties' direct marketing purposes. The Practice does not disclose personal information to third parties for their direct marketing purposes. Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the Practice using the information in Section 15.

13. Children's Privacy

The Website is not directed to children under the age of 13, and the Practice does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the Website. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to the Practice through the Website, please contact the Practice so that the information may be deleted. Information regarding pediatric patients is handled in accordance with the Practice's Notice of Privacy Practices and applicable law.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Practice may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be effective upon posting of the revised Privacy Policy on the Website, as indicated by the updated Effective Date. The Practice encourages you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Your continued use of the Website after such posting constitutes your acknowledgment of the revised Privacy Policy.
